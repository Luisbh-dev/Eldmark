# Licenses

Eldmark mixes three kinds of material. Each has its own license.

| Material | License |
|---|---|
| Source code (GDScript, Go, shaders, tests, build tools, scripts that generate assets) | MIT, see [LICENSE](LICENSE) |
| Original assets made by the project | CC BY 4.0, see below |
| Third-party assets and addons | Their own license, see the table below |

## Original assets (CC BY 4.0)

Assets created by the Eldmark project are licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
You may share and adapt them, including commercially, as long as you give credit.

Suggested attribution:

> Eldmark contributors (https://github.com/Luisbh-dev/Eldmark), CC BY 4.0

### What counts as an original asset

- `assets/audio/sfx/**`: the 89 sound effects synthesized with `tools/audio/synth_sfx.py`, and
  `assets/audio/sfx/niveles.json`. **Exception:** `assets/audio/sfx/step_grass/` and
  `assets/audio/sfx/step_wood/` (when they arrive from the pending branch) are third-party
  (Kenney, CC0) and are not CC BY 4.0.
- `assets/ui/icons/**` (including `small/` and `backgrounds/`): generated by `tools/ui/icons/`.
- `assets/emblems/*.svg`: generated by `tools/assets/make_emblems.py` (provisional).
- `assets/models/weapons/*.glb`: swords built with `tools/blender/build_sword.py` and `sword_textures.py`.
- `assets/models/environment/custom/`: `fort_keep.glb` and its `textures/` and `materials/`, plus
  `stone_bridge.tres`, `fort_gate_leaf.tres`, `fire_pit_logs.tres`, `fire_pit_stones.tres` and
  `rubble.tres` (own geometry; `stone_bridge` references CC0 textures from the Castle Wall Kit).
- `assets/materials/**`: shaders and materials (realm surfaces, water, fort keep, sky, vegetation).
  They recolor CC0 third-party textures by reference; the textures themselves are not relicensed.
- `assets/environments/vado_gris_frontier.tres`.
- `assets/maps/**`: zone maps, minimap and parchment, baked with `tools/bake_zone_map.gd` and `tools/map/*`.
- `assets/ui/theme/eldmark_theme.tres`.
- `assets/animations/rig/ual_humanoid_bone_map.tres`.
- `assets/models/characters/**/materials/*.tres`.
- `assets/textures/terrain/watercolor/flat_normal_rough_256.png`.
- `scenes/vfx/**`: textures from `tools/vfx/make_vfx_textures.py`, hand-written shaders, materials and scenes.
- `scenes/world/**`: prop and zone scenes, Terrain3D region data generated by
  `tools/terrain/generate_vado_gris.gd`, and navigation meshes. They reference third-party CC0
  meshes, which keep their own license.
- `docs/world_map.svg` and `docs/screenshots/*`: the world map and in-game screenshots.

Scripts under `tools/` that generate these assets are code, so they are MIT.

### Derived third-party files (not CC BY 4.0)

These files are modified copies of third-party CC0 material. They stay CC0 and must not be
presented as original work (label them "CC0, modified"):

- `assets/models/characters/base/Superhero_Male_Head.*` (Quaternius, cropped)
- `assets/models/characters/outfits/ranger/Male_Ranger.bin` (collar fixed)
- `assets/models/environment/village/meshes/*.tres`, `assets/models/environment/castle_wall/meshes/*.tres`
  and `assets/models/blockout/kenney_castle/meshes/*.tres` (meshes extracted from the packs)
- `assets/textures/terrain/watercolor/*_alb_ht.png` (height in alpha, one recolored)
- Reduced-resolution textures in the Quaternius packs listed below

## Pending license review

Nothing in `main` is blocked, but these items must be settled before they are described as
fully cleared:

- **Castle Wall Kit (loafbrr).** The itch.io license field says CC0 1.0, but the `README.txt`
  in the original archive does not name the license. A saved copy of the itch.io page is still
  pending. Until then it is listed as CC0 on the strength of the itch.io field only.
- **Modular Character Outfits Fantasy.** The exact pack version is still unconfirmed in
  `assets/LICENCIAS.md`. This does not change the license (CC0).
- **Derived files.** The modified third-party files listed above must be labelled "CC0, modified"
  wherever they are credited.
- **Material in the pending quality-plan branch** (not in `main` yet). It must be declared when
  it lands: Poly Haven terrain textures (CC0), Kenney Impact Sounds (CC0, in
  `assets/audio/sfx/step_grass/` and `step_wood/`), and the Cinzel, Noto Sans and IM Fell
  English SC fonts (SIL OFL 1.1, `assets/fonts/`). The fonts are **not** MIT or CC BY 4.0:
  `assets/fonts/` must be excluded from the CC BY 4.0 grant, and the `OFL-*.txt` files must stay
  next to the fonts, also in exported games.
