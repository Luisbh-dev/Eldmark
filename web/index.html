I

The legend of the Broken Crown

Eld, the one kingdom

Three centuries ago the three peoples were a single kingdom, ruled from the citadel of Aldcor, where the Flame of Eld burned: an ancient fire that made the land fertile and bound the three lineages by oath.

II

The last king

The last king died without an heir. The three lineages claimed the throne, and the oath that had bound them for generations began to fray.

III

The Sundering

In the battle of Aldcor the Flame burst. The citadel sank, the land cracked open, and each people shut itself behind a wall, blaming the others.

IV

The Embers of Eld

Shards of the Flame still burn among the ruins and grant power to whoever holds them. It is said that whoever gathers enough of them will rekindle the Flame and wear the Broken Crown.

The war zone is the corpse of the kingdom they once were: a shared past that only one can inherit.

You fight for

No diplomacy, no cross-realm chat: of an enemy you see the body, the banner and, when you aim at them, the name. Never the voice.

Eldrun, the war zone

Take the forts. Hold the lines.

Six forts, three castles and the ruined citadel of Aldcor, joined by the Ember Lines. You can only attack what your chain of strongholds reaches; cut an enemy’s chain and everything behind it is isolated. Press play and watch a war unfold by the real rules.

Forts

Small, quick objectives. Break the gate, kill the captain, then hold the circle under the standard for 30 seconds. More allies capture faster; any enemy inside freezes it.

Castles

Velmont, Grauholt and Sarnath: walls, gates and several lines of defense, one between each pair of realms. Siege weapons arrive with the full war zone.

Aldcor

The Broken Throne. Only a realm that holds two of the three castles may attack it. Whoever takes it rekindles the Flame for a few hours, while the other two strike back with a bonus.

Action combat

Aim it. Time it. Earn it.

No target lock and no tab-targeting: a swing lands if the blade’s arc touches the enemy. Over-the-shoulder camera, crosshair aiming, blocking and dodging, all paid for with stamina.

  • Light combo, charged heavyLMB

    Click to chain light slashes; hold to charge a heavy blow that breaks guards.

  • Block and parryRMB

    Raise the shield to block. Tap it just before the hit to parry and stagger the attacker.

  • DodgeCtrl

    Roll in the direction you are moving, with invulnerability frames.

  • The bowQ

    Quick shots and fully drawn shots, with projectiles simulated deterministically on the server.

  • Weapon abilities1 2 3

    Shield Bash, Shield Rush and Shield Wall; Volley, Evasive Shot and Slowing Shot. Each weapon brings three.

  • Downed, not deadHold E

    A fallen fighter can still be revived by an ally, or finished off by an enemy.

Open source

Forge it with us

MIT for the code, CC BY 4.0 for the art and sound the project makes. Clone it, study it, break it, or build your own realm on top of it.

Play with a local server 
# Godot 4.7, Git and Git LFS
git clone https://github.com/Luisbh-dev/Eldmark
cd Eldmark
git lfs install && git lfs pull
godot --headless --path . --import
# starts a local server if none is running, then connects
godot --path . -- --local --realm=vardheim --name=Ana
